Frequently asked questions

Beginning can feel like a big step.

How do I know if therapy is right for me?+

Therapy can be useful when something feels difficult, stuck, or simply worth understanding more deeply. A complimentary consultation is a good opportunity to ask questions and see whether working together feels like the right fit.

What can I expect in our first session?+

Our first session is a conversation. We will discuss what brings you in, what you hope might change, and any questions you have about the process. There is no need to prepare or have the right words.

Do you offer virtual appointments?+

Yes. Secure virtual sessions are available to clients located in the state where I am licensed. In-person appointments are also available in New York City.

Do you accept insurance?+

Please reach out to discuss current insurance and out-of-network options. I can provide a superbill for eligible reimbursement when appropriate.
